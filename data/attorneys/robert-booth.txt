Representative Trials and Appeals:
-	Appeal (2026): 26-20095—United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit

Robert represented a national athletic apparel retailer in an appeal from a summary judgment dismissing a customer's negligence and premises liability claims arising from a trip-and-fall in one of the retailer's Houston-area stores. The Fifth Circuit affirmed for substantially the reasons stated by the district court, describing its ruling as "thoughtful and well-reasoned."

-	Jury Trial (2026): 24-CV-2169; 122nd District Court of Galveston County, Texas (Judge J. Jones)

Robert was lead lawyer defending the purchaser of a Galveston County residence at a foreclosure sale against a title challenge by the property's former owner, which sought more than $1 million in damages, and two intervenors, including a national mortgage loan trust, which sought to void the foreclosure sale and quiet title against Robert's client. During the three-day jury trial, the Court granted a directed verdict on every claim against Robert's client other than the competing claims for declaratory relief, and the jury rejected every liability theory submitted and found that the former owner had procured the release of lien underlying its claims by fraud. The Court entered judgment declaring that Robert's client holds good and indefeasible title to the property, ordering that the plaintiff and intervenors take nothing, and awarding Robert's client $33,712 in attorney's fees plus conditional appellate fees. The plaintiff and the mortgage trust have appealed; Robert is defending the judgment on appeal.

-	Bench Trial (2026): 21-CV-0375; 405th District Court of Galveston County, Texas (Judge J. Ellisor)

Robert was lead lawyer defending a former oilfield pipe distributor sued by a city seeking to recover approximately $974,000 in sales-tax rebates paid under a Chapter 380 economic development agreement, plus interest and attorney's fees. The city sought repayment after the Texas Comptroller reallocated the underlying sales taxes in 2021, four years after Robert's client had closed its facility in the city. After a one-day bench trial, the Court ruled that the city take nothing, found Robert's client to be the prevailing party, and awarded Robert's client $97,318 in attorney's fees on its third-party claims. The city has appealed, and Robert is defending the judgment in the Fourteenth Court of Appeals.

-	Jury Trial (2025): CV-0089899; County Court at Law No. 1 of Galveston County, Texas (Judge J. Grady)

The jury found a breach of contract and awarded the requested rent credit of $328,744. Attorneys fees awarded for a total judgment of ~$414,000.00.
